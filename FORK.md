## Why this fork exists

The Lling0000 fork keeps the academic skill collection easy to find from the Lling0000 account and makes the GitHub front door clearer for people evaluating whether the repo is useful.

This fork should be read as a curated/rehosted edition, not as a claim that Lling0000 created the upstream project.
